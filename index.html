About Our Dairy

Built Around Quality

We believe that good milk starts with careful sourcing. Our dairy follows strict quality preferences while working with suppliers for consistent milk quality.

Our Milk Quality Policy

We strictly purchase good-quality milk. Our preferred milk quality standard requires SNF above 98. We focus on maintaining consistent quality rather than accepting milk that does not meet our requirements.


We also avoid cow milk as part of our procurement preference.
